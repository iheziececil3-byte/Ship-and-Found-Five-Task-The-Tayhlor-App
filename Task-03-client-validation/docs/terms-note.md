# Terms and Conditions Note

The terms and conditions checkbox on the registration form is mandatory.

**Where the wording came from:**
I did not write the legal text from scratch, and I did not ask the AI agent to invent it. 

The base template was adapted from the TailorsPA Terms & Conditions (tailorspa.com/terms), a software platform for bespoke fashion designers owned by a Nigerian company and governed by Nigerian law (NDPR). 

I modified the template to fit Tayhlor's specific requirements:
- Removed SaaS subscription/Free plan language (Tayhlor is a single-shop deployment).
- Added the 70% deposit / 30% balance payment gate rules.
- Added the manual refund process clause.
- Clarified the single Tailor (admin) role as Data Controller.
- Added the AI FAQ assistant disclaimer.
- **Added the Sub-processors clause** to explicitly name Vercel PostgreSQL, Paystack, Resend, and Google Gemini, as required by data privacy transparency rules.
- **Added Suspension, Liability, Indemnity, and Changes to Terms** to ensure the business owner is legally protected against misuse and client disputes.

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 rights and lawful bases were retained from the template, as they apply directly to Tayhlor's data collection (measurements, email, phone, payment records).
